## A second look

This batch is not whole files. Each block is one function or method that an earlier reading of
its file recorded nothing for. Look at each again. Most functions a request, command or caller
reaches do something a user, operator or calling system could notice, even when they only hand
the work on: what they accept, what they refuse and with which error, what they hand on, and
what they return. Record that. A function that really holds nothing worth recording goes under
`none`, with its file path and a reason that names the function.
